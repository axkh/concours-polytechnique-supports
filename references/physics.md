## Physics References

The **MPSI–MP2I** volume is used for fundamental first-year material, while the **MP/MP\*–MPI/MPI\*** volume is used for advanced second-year topics. The order is thematic and should not be treated as a study roadmap.

1. **Physical Constants and Orders of Magnitude**

2. **Mathematical Tools for Physics**

3. **Vector Calculus and Differential Operators**

4. **Differential Equations and Linear Algebra**

5. **Trigonometry and Fourier Series**

6. **Mechanics**

7. **Electric Circuits**

8. **Electrostatics**

9. **Magnetostatics and Electromagnetic Induction**

10. **Maxwell's Equations and Electromagnetic Waves**

11. **Geometrical Optics**

12. **Wave Optics**

13. **Thermodynamics**

14. **Introduction to Quantum Physics**

## Useful Online Resources for Physics

The following websites provide lecture notes, exercises, worked solutions, and supplementary materials for physics in preparatory classes.

- **Olivier Granier's Physics Resources**  
  Course notes, exercises, and physics materials for CPGE students.  
  <http://olivier.granier.free.fr>

- **Alain Lerille's Physics Sheets**  
  Revision sheets, summaries, and supplementary course materials.  
  <http://alain.lerille.free.fr>

- **Physique PT Cluny**  
  Physics courses, exercises, and resources for preparatory classes.  
  <https://physique-pt-cluny.monsite-orange.fr>

- **CPGE Kléber Physics Resources**  
  Lecture notes and physics materials for first-year preparatory classes.  
  <https://jjfpcsi1.github.io/CPGE-Kleber/physique.html>

- **Femto-Physique**  
  Online physics courses, explanations, simulations, and exercises.  
  <https://femto-physique.fr/>

- **MPSI Louis-le-Grand Physics Resources**  
  Physics course notes, exercise sheets, and additional materials for MPSI students at Lycée Louis-le-Grand.  
  <http://mpsi2llg.free.fr/mpsi2-physique.html>

> **Remark:** For information about obtaining the books listed above, or for additional references and learning resources, please contact the AXK Facebook page.
