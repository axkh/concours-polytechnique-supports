## Mathematics References

The following references are organized by topic. Chapter references are provided to help the reader locate the relevant material directly. The **MP/MP\*** volume is used for advanced topics. The order is thematic and should not be treated as a study roadmap.

1. **Set Theory**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 0 and 6–7.

2. **Numbers and Algebraic Structures**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 1, 3, and 15–18.  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapter 1.

3. **Finite-Dimensional Vector Spaces**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 19–24.

4. **Reduction of Endomorphisms**  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapter 2.

5. **Euclidean Spaces and Geometry**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 25–27.  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapter 14.

6. **Topology in Finite-Dimensional Normed Spaces**  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapters 4–6.  
   See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapter 1.

7. **Functions of One Variable**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 4, 8–10, and 13.  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapters 3 and 7.  
   See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapter 2.

8. **Integration**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapters 11–12.  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapters 12–13.  
   See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapters 2–3.

9. **Series and Power Series**  
   Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapter 14.  
   Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapters 8–11.  
   See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapters 4–7.

10. **Differential Equations**  
    Deschamps et al., *Maths MPSI tout-en-un*, Chapter 5.  
    Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapter 17.  
    See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapter 8.

11. **Functions of Several Variables and Differential Calculus**  
    Deschamps et al., *Mathématiques MP/MP\* tout-en-un*, Chapter 18.  
    See also Monier, *Analyse MP*, Chapter 9.
