# École Polytechnique Entrance Exam Support

This is a community support space for candidates preparing for the **École Polytechnique International Entrance Exam**, especially the **Filière Universitaire Internationale (FUI)**.

It is maintained by the **Association des Polytechniciens Khmers (AXK)** to help Cambodian candidates understand the exam, organize their preparation, share resources, and discuss mathematics, physics, application requirements, and oral examinations.

## Purpose of this project

The goal is to provide a long-term preparation and discussion platform for future Cambodian candidates.

This project aims to:

- collect useful information about the École Polytechnique entrance exam
- organize recommended background knowledge in mathematics and physics
- provide reference links, books, lecture notes, and study resources
- help candidates ask questions and discuss preparation strategies
- support Cambodian candidates interested in École Polytechnique.

### Preparation document

The main preparation document organizes the official information and recommended background knowledge into a clean study guide.

[FUI 2027 Preparation PDF](summaries/fui-2027-preparation-summary.pdf)

## Main discussion platform

We use **GitHub Discussions** as the main public discussion space:

[Go to Discussions](https://github.com/axkh/concours-polytechnique-supports/discussions)

Candidates can use the discussion space to ask questions about:

- General infomation related to exam
- mathematics preparation
- physics preparation
- oral examination
- reference books and study resources
- study planning and preparation advice.

A GitHub account is required to post questions, reply, react, or participate in discussions.

---

## Suggested discussion categories

The discussion space is organized into several categories:

- **Announcements**  
  Important updates from AXK or project maintainers.

- **Exam Information**  
  Questions about the official notice, eligibility, application documents, deadlines, and exam process.

- **Mathematics**  
  Questions and resources related to the mathematics syllabus.

- **Physics**  
  Questions and resources related to the physics syllabus.

- **Oral Exams**  
  Preparation for scientific oral examinations, motivation discussion, and interview practice.

- **Resources**  
  Books, lecture notes, websites, videos, exercises, and useful links.

- **General Discussion**  
  General questions, study groups, and candidate support.

## How to ask a good question

When asking a question, please include enough context so others can help you effectively.

Recommended format:

```markdown
### Topic

Write the topic of your question.

### Question

Explain clearly what you want to know.
```

Optionals:

```markdown
### What I already checked

Mention the official notice, book, lecture note, exercise, or discussion you already looked at.

### Where I am stuck

Explain the specific difficulty.

### Screenshot or reference

Add a screenshot, link, page number, or formula if useful. Maths and Pyshics, Latex form is prefered.
```

## Rules for discussions

To keep the platform useful for future candidates:

1. Be respectful.
2. Search existing discussions before asking a new question.
3. Give context when asking technical questions.
4. Do not share illegal copies of books or copyrighted materials.
5. Do not present unofficial information as official.
6. Always refer candidates to the official website for final confirmation.
7. Keep answers educational and constructive.
